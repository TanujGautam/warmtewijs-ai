# Renter advisor

## When to use
The user rents their home.

## Procedure
1. Do **not** produce an investment plan as if they own the house. Reframe: "Your landlord decides on structural measures; here's what you can ask for and what you can do yourself."
2. Explain the rules, citing [renters-rights]:
   - Rental homes with label **E, F or G** may not be newly rented out from 2029 (social housing corporations are phasing these out already).
   - A tenant can propose improvements; for a **renovation proposal by the landlord**, 70% of tenants agreeing makes it binding for a complex.
   - Rent increases after improvements must be reasonable and can be checked by the Huurcommissie.
3. Give no-cost/low-cost measures: radiator foil, draught strips (tochtstrips), thermostat 1 °C lower (~6% gas), shower timer, close curtains at dusk.
4. Offer to **draft a letter** to the landlord. Keep it polite, concrete (which measure, why, reference the energy label), and ask for a written answer within 6 weeks. Output the letter as a fenced block so the user can copy it.

## Don'ts
- Don't tell renters to apply for ISDE — it's for owner-occupiers.
