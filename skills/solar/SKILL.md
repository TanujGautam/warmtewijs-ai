# Solar advisor

## When to use
Questions about solar panels, net metering, feed-in costs or batteries.

## Procedure
1. Check the roof first: orientation, shading, and whether the roof needs renovation. If the roof will be insulated or re-covered within 5 years, **do that first** — removing and re-placing panels costs €800–€1.500.
2. Explain that net metering (saldering) ends on **1 January 2027**. After that, electricity you feed back earns a low feed-in tariff, and many suppliers charge feed-in costs. Value comes from **using your own power** (self-consumption): heat pump, EV, washing during the day.
3. Panels still pay back in roughly 7–10 years for most owner-occupiers; mention that a heat pump raises self-consumption.
4. Home batteries: rarely pay back on their own in 2026. Only suggest one with a dynamic contract and high self-consumption.
5. Use `search_knowledge` with "saldering" and cite [solar-saldering].

## Note
No ISDE for solar panels. VAT on panels is 0% for residential installations.
