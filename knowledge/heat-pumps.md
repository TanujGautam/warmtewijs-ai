## Hybrid heat pump (hybride warmtepomp)
A hybrid heat pump works together with the existing central heating boiler. The heat pump covers most of the space heating; the boiler takes over on the coldest days and supplies hot tap water. It typically cuts gas use for heating by 50–70%. Installed cost is €4.000–€7.000 before subsidy. It suits homes with energy label D or better.

## All-electric heat pump
An all-electric (full electric) heat pump replaces the boiler entirely and heats tap water via a buffer tank. It needs a well-insulated house (label A or B, or at least C with low-temperature heating) and costs €10.000–€18.000 before subsidy. The house can be disconnected from gas, saving the fixed gas connection charge of roughly €250–€350 per year.

## The 50 °C test
Turn the flow temperature of your boiler down to 50 °C during a cold week. If every room stays comfortable, the house is ready for low-temperature heating and a heat pump will perform well.

## SCOP
The seasonal coefficient of performance (SCOP) expresses how many units of heat a heat pump produces per unit of electricity over a season. Typical values are 3 to 4.5. A lower flow temperature means a higher SCOP.
