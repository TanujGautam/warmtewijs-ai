## Labels and rental homes
From 2029, rental homes with an energy label E, F or G may not be newly rented out. Housing corporations aim to have no homes with these labels by 2028.

## Asking your landlord
Tenants can ask their landlord in writing to carry out energy improvements. A landlord is not always obliged to agree, but can propose a renovation. For a complex, a renovation proposal becomes binding when at least 70% of tenants agree.

## Rent after improvements
After an improvement, the landlord may ask a reasonable rent increase. Disagreements can be put before the Huurcommissie. Tenants of homes with serious defects such as mould may also request a temporary rent reduction.

## Low-cost measures for tenants
Radiator foil behind radiators on outside walls, draught strips around doors and windows, a thermostat setting one degree lower (about 6% less gas), shorter showers, and closing curtains at dusk all help without the landlord's permission.
