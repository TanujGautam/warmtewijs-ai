## Energiebespaarlening
The Nationaal Warmtefonds offers a loan for energy-saving measures of €1.000 up to €28.000 (more for all-electric packages), with a fixed interest rate and a term of 10, 15 or 20 years. Early repayment is free.

## Interest rates
Households with a gross household income below roughly €60.000 per year can borrow at 0% interest. Above that, a fixed rate applies, indicatively 3–4% in 2026 depending on the term.

## Mortgage top-up
Homeowners can also finance energy measures with an extra mortgage loan. The mortgage norms allow extra borrowing room for energy-saving measures on top of the normal maximum.

## Comparing options
Compare the monthly loan payment to the expected monthly energy saving. When the saving is larger, the measure is cash-flow positive from the first month.
