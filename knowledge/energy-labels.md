## The scale
Dutch energy labels run from G (worst) to A++++ (best), based on the primary fossil energy use per m² per year. Since 2021 labels are issued by a certified advisor who visits the home.

## What moves a label
Cavity wall plus roof insulation typically moves a 1970s terraced house (rijtjeshuis) one or two steps, e.g. from D to C or B. Adding HR++ glass and a hybrid heat pump commonly reaches A. Solar panels also improve the label because they reduce primary fossil energy.

## Why the label matters
A better label increases the market value of a home (studies indicate several percent between D and A) and gives extra mortgage borrowing room (energiebudget) for buyers. For rental homes, labels E–G will be restricted from 2029.

## Energy label lookup
The registered label for an address can be looked up in EP-Online. Many older houses have only an indicative label from 2015, which is often inaccurate.
