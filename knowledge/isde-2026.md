## Who can apply
ISDE (Investeringssubsidie duurzame energie en energiebesparing) is for owner-occupiers who live in the house, and for VvE's via a separate route (SVVE). Tenants cannot apply. Apply after installation, within 24 months, with the invoice and proof of payment.

## Insulation rates (2025 onwards)
- Cavity wall: €5,25 per m² for one measure, €10,50 per m² with two or more measures. Minimum 10 m², maximum 170 m², Rd-value at least 1,1.
- Roof: €16,25 per m², €32,50 per m² with two or more measures. Minimum 20 m², maximum 200 m², Rd-value at least 3,5.
- Attic floor (zoldervloer): €4 per m², €8 with two measures. Minimum 20 m², Rd at least 3,5; the attic must be unheated.
- Floor: €5,50 per m², €11 per m² with two or more measures. Minimum 20 m², maximum 130 m², Rd-value at least 3,5.
- Ground (bodemisolatie): €3 per m², €6 with two measures. Minimum 20 m², Rd at least 3,5.
- Facade (gevelisolatie): €20,25 per m², €40,50 with two measures. Minimum 10 m², Rd at least 3,5. No subsidy for interior walls.
- HR++ glass: €25 per m², €50 per m² with two or more measures. U-value at most 1,2. Minimum 3 m², maximum 45 m².
- Triple glass with insulating frames: €111 per m², €222 with two measures; triple glass U-value at most 0,7.
- Biobased insulation materials earn an extra bonus per m².

## Paperwork
Apply within 24 months after the work is finished, with a photo of the installation, the invoice (with the meldcode of the material or device) and proof of payment. All insulation work must be carried out by a construction or installation company.

## The two-measure rule
If you carry out two or more measures within 24 months — two insulation measures, or one insulation measure plus a heat pump — the insulation rates double. Planning measures together therefore pays.

## Heat pumps
A hybrid heat pump typically receives €1.500–€3.000 depending on capacity; an all-electric heat pump €2.500–€4.500. Only devices on the RVO apparatus list qualify.

## Not covered
Solar panels are not covered by ISDE; they have a 0% VAT rate instead.
