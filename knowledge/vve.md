## Building-level decisions
In an apartment building, the facade, roof and shared installations belong to the VvE. Measures such as cavity wall or roof insulation need a decision in the general meeting (ledenvergadering), usually by majority.

## SVVE subsidy
The Subsidieregeling Verduurzaming voor VvE's (SVVE) covers part of the cost of a sustainability advice, a long-term maintenance plan (MJOP) update, and insulation and heat pump measures carried out by the VvE.

## MJOP
Combining sustainability measures with planned maintenance in the MJOP (meerjarenonderhoudsplan) is the cheapest route: the scaffolding is already there when the roof or facade is maintained.
