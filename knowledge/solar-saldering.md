## End of saldering
The net metering scheme (salderingsregeling) ends on 1 January 2027. Until then, electricity fed into the grid is offset against electricity used. After 2027 you receive a feed-in tariff that is far lower than the consumer price, and many suppliers charge feed-in costs (terugleverkosten).

## What it means
Solar panels remain worthwhile for most owner-occupiers, but their value depends on self-consumption: using power when the sun shines. A heat pump, electric car or smart appliances increase self-consumption. Indicative payback is 7–10 years.

## Batteries
A home battery stores daytime surplus for the evening. In 2026 batteries rarely pay back purely on savings, but can with a dynamic energy contract.

## Roof first
If the roof needs insulation or renovation within a few years, do that before placing panels to avoid removal and re-installation costs.
